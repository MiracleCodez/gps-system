# Research sources used in the thesis draft

1. **Nyanza District, “About Nyanza District.”** Official district website. Used for the description of Nyanza as a Southern Province district, its 10 sectors, 51 cells, 420 villages, historical/cultural role, and economic activities. https://www.nyanza.gov.rw/en/district/about-the-district

2. **Ministry of Infrastructure, Rwanda, “Transport.”** Official government website. Used for transport, government fleet management, traffic management, mobility, accessibility, reliability, safety, monitoring, and evaluation context. https://www.mininfra.gov.rw/1/transport

3. **University of Lay Adventists of Kigali, “UNILAK Profile.”** Official UNILAK website. Used for UNILAK’s Nyanza Campus, institutional background, mission, vision, and research/community-service context. https://site.unilak.ac.rw/elementor-1875/

## Important fieldwork note

The thesis draft models the existing operational workflow as a manual or semi-manual arrangement involving phone calls, messages, notebooks, spreadsheets, and supervisor follow-up. These workflow descriptions are analytical assumptions for the prototype and must be validated through interviews, observation, questionnaires, or official process documents before final academic submission. The researcher should identify the exact district department or organization studied and replace the assumptions with collected evidence.
